## Operating guidance to Codex

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `data/favorites_source_map.json`, and `specs/favorites.json` before making edits.
2. Read-only inventory and complete backups come **before** modifying Rig Manager. Proposed location 01–05 does not grant permission to overwrite any actual occupied bank.
3. Pilot Favorites 01, storing, reloading and checking all four Remote assignments and their initial effects states.
4. After approval, build Favorites 02–05 in numeric order. Do not erase source Performances 06–48.
5. Perceived output level matching is a listening/calibration task, especially for clean-to-high-gain switching.
6. These files are blueprints, not native Kemper .kipr or .kperform files, and do not establish installed tone fidelity.
